I'm an ML researcher working on generative modelling (especially diffusion and flow models) and probabilistic inference, with a particular emphasis on sampling. My recent work adapts probabilistic methods for post-training and test-time scaling in both continuous and discrete diffusion models, including applications to problems in physics and biology. This is a direction I'm keen to develop further.

I'm currently a PhD student at [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en/), supervised by [Yoshua Bengio](https://yoshuabengio.org/). Previously, I completed my master's thesis on Bayesian federated learning with [Pascal Poupart](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~ppoupart/).
